AI Budtender - Cannabis Marketplace

Founding Product Designer

I was iHeartJane’s sole founding product designer for 1.5 years before hiring a senior designer. I led user research across 30 dispensaries, working directly with dispensary owners.

I owned the ecommerce experience from discovery to purchase: product detail pages, search, map view, cart, and checkout across mobile web, desktop, and in-store kiosks. My scope also included the back-office tools that kept point-of-sale systems and online menus in sync.

This independent AI budtender prototype builds on that foundation. I’m exploring how shoppers can express what they want through activity, feeling, category, and budget, then move directly into relevant products—with chat reserved for order logistics.

How I design and build.

I connect customer needs to product strategy, align teams around a direction, and stay close to the craft through delivery.

  1. 01

    Frame the right problem.

    Bring customer research, product signals, and business goals together. Define the opportunity, constraints, and what success should mean.

    Repository / source of truthProblem, constraints, success criteriaspec.md
  2. 02

    Align on a direction.

    Partner with product, engineering, and leadership to shape a shared vision. Make tradeoffs explicit and focus the team on the most valuable next step.

    Figma <--> RepositoryShared design decisions + Tailwind tokensDESIGN.md
  3. 03

    Explore. Test. Decide.

    Explore multiple paths and prototype the riskiest assumptions. Use customer feedback and feasibility checks to choose what earns further investment.

    Cursor / Codex --> Next.jsComposer + Codex variants · optional v0 CLItask-template.md
  4. 04

    Carry the craft through.

    Guide interaction quality, accessibility, and the shared design system. Work alongside engineers and support designers as the experience takes shape.

    Git branch --> DevinPlaywright QA + reviewable previewdevin-qa.md
  5. 05

    Measure and evolve.

    Compare outcomes with the starting baseline. Combine usage data with customer feedback, then feed what we learn into the next product decision.

    Review --> next briefEvidence, outcomes, next decisionreview-notes.md
